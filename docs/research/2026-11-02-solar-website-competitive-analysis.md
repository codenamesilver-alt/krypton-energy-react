# Competitive UI/UX & Content Analysis — India's Top Solar Panel Websites

**Prepared for:** Krypton Energy / KRYPTON 2.0
**Date:** 2 November 2026
**Scope:** 10 leading Indian solar panel companies
**Method:** Each site loaded in a live Chromium session at 1440×900. Computed styles, typography, palette, navigation, CTA treatments and hero copy were extracted from the rendered DOM (not from screenshots or marketing blurbs). Capacity figures are approximate and come from 2026 industry roundups; verify before publishing.

> Note: Goldi Solar's public homepage returned a WordPress password gate on the audit date. Its tokens below come from its stylesheet, not a live render.

## 2. Site-by-site

### 1. Waaree Energies — waaree.com
**Positioning:** Global scale. Hero is "Accelerating Global Energy Transition" with four proof stats (25.8 GW modules, 5.4 GW cells, 24+ countries, 40 quarters Tier 1). Next block splits products (Modules, Inverters, Turnkey EPC, Green Hydrogen, Storage) then applications.
**Design:** Poppins throughout, light canvas, photography-led, pill buttons (100px radius), hairline grey copy. Green is used sparingly; blue appears in interactive elements.
**Content:** Savings calculator, blogs, whitepapers, events, ESG, investor section, downloadable material.
**Takeaway:** Best-in-class scale proof, but the homepage speaks to analysts and EPC buyers. A homeowner has no obvious entry point beyond the calculator.

### 2. Adani Solar — adanisolar.com
**Positioning:** Vertically integrated manufacturer, nation-building tone. Domain story: polysilicon → wafer → cell → module.
**Design:** The only truly dark site in the set — `#001C24` canvas, white type, custom `Adani-Regular` face, 0px radius. Product taxonomy is by cell format (144 HC, 132 HC) and series (Shine TOPCon, MonoPERC, Pride).
**Content:** Manufacturing walkthrough, technology explainers, sustainability reports, newsroom, downloads, "Ask our expert".
**Takeaway:** Proves a dark, premium canvas works in this category. But the information architecture is engineered for trade buyers; the human story is absent.

### 3. Tata Power Solaroof — tatapower.com/solaroof
**Positioning:** "India's No. 1 rooftop solar company", eight years running. The only site built residential-first.
**Design:** Syne display (48/600) over system sans; cream `#F8F5EC` surfaces, deep teal `#10635A` CTAs at 20px radius, amber `#D68100` as a warm secondary. 100 images — project photography carries the trust.
**Content:** Rooftop categories (Residential, C&I, Institutions), subsidy guidance, calculator, enquiry form, project gallery.
**Takeaway:** The strongest residential reference. Cream + teal is the exact opposite direction from Krypton's charcoal + gold — worth studying for structure, not for skin.

### 4. Vikram Solar — vikramsolar.com
**Positioning:** Engineering-led, export-scale, climate mission.
**Design:** Full-bleed industrial photography, DINOT body / Neo Sans display, all-caps headings, red `#CE181E` accent. Very little radius.
**Content:** Module portfolio (Suryava, Hypersol Pro, Hypersol, Paradea), solutions, projects, channel partner, solar calculator, blogs, downloads.
**Takeaway:** Powerful but cold. All-caps at every level flattens hierarchy; the red reads industrial rather than premium.

### 5. Premier Energies — premierenergies.com
**Positioning:** Integrated cell + module manufacturer, 30 years, investor-facing.
**Design:** Bebas Neue condensed display with Montserrat body; navy `#154074` + green `#65BC46`; white canvas. Dense with numbers (10.6 GW cells, 11.1 GW modules).
**Content:** Products, technology, sustainability, investor relations, news, enquiry.
**Takeaway:** Reads like an annual report. Useful pattern: capacity numbers as a standalone "in numbers" band.

### 6. Goldi Solar — goldisolar.com
**Status:** Public homepage is behind a WordPress password prompt as of 2 Nov 2026. Historical tokens: navy `#152142`, electric yellow `#FFE619`, Gotham/Roboto, WordPress.
**Takeaway:** Revisit before drawing conclusions. The navy/yellow pairing is the closest existing analogue to Krypton's dark + gold, so it is worth a second look once the site is live.

### 7. Rayzon Solar — rayzonsolar.com
**Positioning:** Fast-rising challenger, "India's 2nd largest module supplier", Shark Tank India feature.
**Design:** White canvas, teal `#095763` as the primary, gold `#E5B73B` as a secondary accent, Montserrat throughout, 4px radii. Alternating light/dark bands.
**Content:** The strongest content engine in the set — subsidy guides, ROI calculator, installation cost breakdowns, hybrid system guides, expo guides, newsletters, virtual plant tour.
**Takeaway:** The closest existing use of gold in the category, and the best model for organic acquisition. Their gold is a minor accent; Krypton could make it the main identity.

### 8. Saatvik Green Energy — saatvikgroup.com
**Positioning:** Mid-tier challenger scaling to 4.8 GW, full product ecosystem (modules, inverters, kits, pumps, BESS, transformers) plus EPC.
**Design:** The boldest typography in the set — Syne 72/700 white on dark `#171717`, orange `#EA7124`, zero radius, hard alternation between charcoal and white sections.
**Content:** Products, EPC solutions, other solutions, newsroom, investors.
**Takeaway:** Best example of editorial confidence and section rhythm. Orange-on-charcoal is close in *structure* to gold-on-charcoal — study the rhythm, not the hue.

### 9. Emmvee — emmvee.com
**Positioning:** Established manufacturer with India + USA footprint, modules and solar water heaters.
**Design:** Poppins, navy `#030F27`, orange `#F4A32E`, 30px pill buttons, conventional corporate layout.
**Content:** About, manufacturing, products, turnkey solutions, media, downloads, certifications, contact.
**Takeaway:** Competent and forgettable. The clearest example of what to avoid: stock corporate template with no distinct point of view.

### 10. Loom Solar — loomsolar.com
**Positioning:** India's No. 1 consumer solar brand. D2C e-commerce, dealer/distributor network, installation services.
**Design:** Shopify. Barlow + Inter, green `#08B85A` and deep green `#072B20`, white canvas, mixed radii (2–30px). Product cards, ratings, offers, bilingual toggle.
**Content:** Hero "You can save up to 80%* on monthly electricity bills. Get a quote!"; product ecosystem; why-choose-us; real customer installations; logo wall; bill-based system finder; dealer and distributor registration; AMC, installation, insurance, O&M services; 7 forms on the homepage.
**Takeaway:** The only genuine homeowner conversion funnel in the top 10. Everything Krypton needs for residential — calculator, city/service pages, social proof, partner recruitment — exists here in one place.

## 3. What is table stakes

1. **A capacity/proof band** — GW manufactured, countries served, years, certifications, Tier 1/ALMM.
2. **Segmented portfolio** — modules by technology (TOPCon, MonoPERC, bifacial) and by application (rooftop, ground-mount, floating, BIPV).
3. **Downloads** — datasheets, warranty PDFs, certificates.
4. **A contact/enquiry path** — form, phone, WhatsApp.
5. **Investor relations** — 7 of 9 live sites have it. This is a manufacturer signal, not a consumer one.
6. **A blog/knowledge hub** — subsidy, cost, ROI, net metering.
7. **A project gallery** — usually utility-scale, rarely residential.

## 4. Where the category is weak (Krypton's opening)

| Gap | Evidence | Opportunity |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| **No local relevance** | Every site is national or global. None names Bihar, Delhi or Dehradun. | City landing pages with local proof, DISCOM guidance, and local install galleries. |
| **Manufacturer-first IA** | "Investors" sits in the top nav of 7/9 sites; residential is one item among many. | A true dual-track homepage: Home vs Business, chosen in the first screen. |
| **Subsidy anxiety unresolved** | PM Surya Ghar mentioned on Rayzon/Saatvik only; Tata buries it. | A step-by-step subsidy + net-metering journey with documents, timelines and FAQ. |
| **No pricing transparency** | Zero sites publish system price bands. | "What a 3 kW system costs in Patna" — the highest-intent page nobody owns. |
| **Proof is industrial** | Case studies are 450 MWp Bikaner, 51 MW Denmark. | Residential before/after stories with bills, roof type, family, locality. |
| **Weak service story** | Only Loom sells AMC, insurance, health checks as products. | Service plans as a first-class section — the post-install relationship is unclaimed. |
| **Gold is unused** | Green/blue/orange/red/teal dominate. | Own "premium solar" visually before a competitor does. |
| **Hindi is absent** | Only Loom has a language toggle. | Hindi/English toggle for Bihar and Delhi NCR. |

## 5. Implications for Krypton's identity

### Palette
- `#1E222A` as the base is a strong, differentiated choice — the only comparable dark canvas is Adani's teal and Saatvik's near-black. It reads premium and makes gold legible.
- **Contrast math (WCAG):**
  - `#E4D18E` on `#1E222A` = **10.5:1** — safe for headlines, body copy, and as a button fill with dark text.
  - `#B48C48` on `#1E222A` = **5.1:1** — passes AA for normal text, good for secondary copy, rules, icons.
  - White on `#B48C48` = **3.1:1** — fails AA for normal text. Do not use white body text on mid-gold; use dark text on gold fills, or reserve white for large text only.
  - `#B48C48` on white = **3.1:1** — reserve for large text, borders and icons, not paragraphs.
- Practical rule: **light gold fills with charcoal text** for primary CTAs; mid-gold for outlines and dividers; charcoal for the page canvas.
- A light surface is still needed for long-form content (subsidy guides, blogs, product specs). Choose a paper tone deliberately and check it against Tata's cream `#F8F5EC` so the site does not read as a Tata derivative.

### Typography
- Poppins appears on 4 sites, Montserrat on 2, Syne on 2. All are category defaults now.
- Syne is the emerging category display face (Tata, Saatvik). Using it would place Krypton inside the trend rather than ahead of it.
- A high-contrast serif or a grotesque with unusual widths would separate Krypton from every competitor. Whatever is chosen, the gold palette already supplies the warmth; the type can stay cool and precise.

### Structure worth copying
- Loom: bill-based system finder, customer installation gallery, dealer/distributor recruitment, service menu.
- Tata: residential-first segmentation, subsidy explainer, enquiry flow.
- Rayzon: subsidy/cost/ROI content engine, virtual tour.
- Saatvik: alternating charcoal/white section rhythm and oversized editorial headlines.
- Waaree: four-stat proof band and application-based product split.

### Structure worth avoiding
- Emmvee: generic corporate template with no point of view.
- Premier: annual-report density on the homepage.
- Vikram: all-caps everywhere, red industrial accent.
- Any site that puts "Investors" above "Residential" in the primary navigation.
