## What to extract from each (analysis frame)

1. **Hero proposition** — what they lead with (capacity, efficiency, savings, nation-building, warranty).
2. **Navigation model** — segment-first (Residential / Commercial / Utility) vs. product-first vs. investor-first.
3. **Proof architecture** — certifications, ALMM, Bloomberg Tier 1, test labs, plant photos, customer logos, numbers.
4. **Conversion path** — calculator, quote form, dealer locator, WhatsApp, e-commerce checkout, phone number prominence.
5. **Content depth** — blog/SEO, case studies, datasheets, warranty documents, installer training.
6. **Visual language** — palette, type system, photography vs. 3D render vs. illustration, motion, trust signals.
7. **Mobile/residential experience** — how a homeowner with a 3 kW roof requirement is served vs. an EPC buyer.
