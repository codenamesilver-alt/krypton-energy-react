03 / The paperwork

Four documents,
in this order.

The subsidy is not complicated, it is sequenced. We file every step; you sign twice. Nothing gets installed before the feasibility letter.

01 Day 1

Application

PM Surya Ghar portal + DISCOM form

We create the application with your consumer number and Aadhaar-linked bank details. You sign once.

02 7–21 days

Feasibility

Sanctioned load approval

The DISCOM confirms how much roof capacity your connection can carry. This is the number that decides your system size.

03 2–4 weeks

Net meter

Bi-directional meter, sealed

Fitted and sealed after commissioning. Until it is sealed, you are still buying every unit at full tariff.

04 30–60 days

Subsidy

Direct benefit transfer

Credited to the bank account on the application after the first meter reading. We chase it, not you.

04 / After

Then we stay.

The panel warranty is 25 years. The relationship has to last at least that long, so service is a schedule, not a phone number.

1year

Monitoring & first clean

Generation checked against the design estimate. Panels cleaned after the first monsoon.

3years

Inverter service

Thermal check, firmware, string readings. Inverter is the part that fails first.

5years

Full audit

Mounting torque, cable insulation, earthing resistance, battery health where fitted.

10years

Performance test

Measured output against day-one. If a module has drifted, we file the warranty claim.
