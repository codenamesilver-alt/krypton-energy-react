From roof to meter

Four steps. No surprises in between.

The part most installers skip is the paperwork and the net meter. We treat both as part of the job, not as your problem.

STEP 01

Survey

Roof measurement, shading, structure check and a read of your last 12 months of bills.

STEP 02

Design & approvals

System layout, generation estimate and the DISCOM application, filed for you.

STEP 03

Installation

Our own crew, typically two to four days, with the meter and wiring signed off.

STEP 04

Net meter & service

Commissioning, subsidy credit, monitoring setup and a service plan that outlasts the warranty.
